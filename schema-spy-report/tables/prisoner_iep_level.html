prisoner_iep_level

-1 rows


Description

One incentive (IEP) review of one prisoner, in an ordered history - not one row per prisoner. A prisoner accumulates a row per review over time and the one that counts today is flagged by current. Holds reviews conducted in DPS alongside the full history migrated from NOMIS, which is why reviewed_by and location_id are nullable and why review_type distinguishes MIGRATED rows. The Kotlin entity is IncentiveReview; the table keeps the legacy NOMIS name.
